SEC/001 — Protocol

  1. Four moves.

    One loan, start to finish.

  2. 01Publish

    The borrower writes the terms and signs. The collateral is worth more than the loan: if the price swings, it holds.

  3. 02Fund

    A lender accepts. The bitcoin lock into the contract, the dollars reach the borrower. A small origination fee goes to the lender.

  4. Time passes.

    Block after block. The price rises or falls: the contract never looks.

  5. 03Repay

    Before the deadline the borrower returns the dollars, plus a flat fee known from day one. A slice stays with the protocol.

  6. 03Repay

    The contract opens and the bitcoin go back to the borrower. Done.

  7. Or.

    Same contract. This time the borrower does not repay.

  8. Time passes.

    Every block, down to the last. Before the deadline nobody can touch the bitcoin.

  9. 04Claim

    Deadline. Now, and only now, the lender takes the bitcoin. Worth more than the loan: that is what protects them.

  10. Four moves. Nothing else.

    No custodian. No oracle. Only time.

SEC/003 — Security

The keys stay in your wallet.

Who has the keys?You. Always.

The site builds the transactions and relays them to the network. It does not custody, does not sign, cannot move the collateral. Every move is a transaction your wallet shows in full and signs. How it connects →

What does the site see?Only addresses. Never a key.

Share

  • A watch-only descriptor: the site sees the addresses, cannot spend.
  • The escrow public key.

Never

  • The seed and the private keys.
  • Passwords or PINs. With Blockstream Jade the key never even touches the computer.
If the site disappears?The contract stays on the chain.

The collateral is locked by a script with the contract's branches and the deadline as a block height. Anyone can verify it; the amounts stay blinded. Your wallet can sign the exit even without the site.

In development Testnet and regtest, no real funds. The wallet and the connector are public: repository, documentation.

SEC/004 — Costs

Three lines. No surprises.

Loan fee

flat · at repayment · in USDt

Details

Written in the offer, it does not grow with time. It is the whole cost of the loan for the term.

Origination fee

once · at origination · in L-BTC

Details

Locked together with the collateral when you publish the offer: it goes to the lender the moment the contract starts.

Protocol fee

a slice of the loan fee · at repayment

Details

Never from the principal. It is not added to the borrower's bill: it comes out of what the lender receives.

No spread · no margin · no penalties
